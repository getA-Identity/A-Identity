A-Identity now checks card spending

Merchant, category and per-card limits, on the same engine that checks trades.

**ATM cash, crypto purchases on the card, and gambling are off by default.** On a card an agent drives, those three turn a spending mistake into one you cannot undo.

Every verdict arrives with a reason and a record. A refusal cannot be overwritten, and attempts to overwrite one are counted.
